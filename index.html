About

Hi, I’m Mohammed. I’m a detail-oriented UI/UX designer with over 18 months of professional experience, and I came into interface design from a strong graphic design and visual communication background. Figma is where I do most of my work. When a client or a team brings me their ideas and requirements, I turn them into clean, functional interfaces that people actually enjoy using.

I graduated from Afe Babalola University, Ado-Ekiti, in 2025 with a B.Sc. in Computer Science, First Class Honours. Right now I’m looking for a UI/UX Designer role, ideally remote or hybrid, where I can work with a team and keep creating meaningful digital experiences.
